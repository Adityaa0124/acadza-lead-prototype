## Chosen translation

Keep the light shell, navy identity, blue navigation and restrained orange accent. Use blue consistently for staff primary actions. Do not transfer promotional banners, rainbow cards or gradient dialog headers to operational workflows.

An earlier kit mentioned public-site navy #1D3564, gold #F4B23A and Lexend/Nunito/Roboto. That source inspection is not independently evidenced in the supplied files. Preserve them here as historical claims rather than requirements. The new kit does not assert that these are the authenticated product's font or action tokens.
