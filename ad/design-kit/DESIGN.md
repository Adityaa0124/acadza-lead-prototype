# Design direction

## Product purpose

Help a clerk record an enquiry accurately and a caller complete the next conversation confidently. Staff should understand what to do without remembering previous screens or knowing software terminology.

## Firm rules

1. One visually dominant action for the current task. A page footer and a repeated sticky copy must represent the same action.
2. Show contact, last relevant conversation, callback commitment and next step together when calling.
3. Show secondary information on demand. Do not hide essential context merely to make a screen look sparse.
4. Use plain labelled actions. Stage, latest outcome, owner and next task remain separate concepts, but do not repeat each concept in multiple panels.
5. Prefill known school/year, allocate work automatically and preserve drafts on failure.
6. Use colour for meaning and selection. No decorative charts, gradients, large greeting banners, nested card grids or illustration filler.
7. Keep common decisions visible. Show four common call outcomes; place less frequent outcomes behind “More outcomes”.
8. Ask for confirmation on closure, reset and bulk reassignment. Routine saving needs clear feedback, not a second confirmation.
9. Defaults are safe and visible: no preselected call outcome, no guessed agreement from a parent, no assumed completed admission.
10. Use named workflow actions instead of an unrestricted status dropdown.

## Visual direction

Light neutral workspace, white working surfaces, navy text, blue primary actions and pale-blue selection. Orange is a narrow brand accent, never a second competing primary-action colour. This follows the supplied product captures. Teal and gold are not additional action colours.

Use one font stack until the real product font is verified. Use readable labels, generous line height and moderate spacing. Keep normal content at 14px or larger; 12px is limited to supplementary metadata.

## Information budget

- Default directory: five meaningful columns plus row action; optional columns in a labelled Columns control.
- Default filter bar: search, stage and Filters. Applied filters stay visible.
- Caller: small queue beside one coherent conversation form; no permanent third panel.
- Routine capture: required information first; optional fields under More details.
- Page heading: title, short useful description and primary action; no mandatory metrics row.
- Long timelines: latest relevant entries first, with View older activity.
- Every hidden option has a predictable text-labelled entry point.

These are defaults, not arbitrary limits that justify hiding necessary warnings or appointments.

## Review standard

A representative clerk must create and find an enquiry without coaching. A caller must identify whom to call, understand the prior conversation, schedule a requested callback and recover from a failed save. Observed hesitation is a design defect to investigate, not proof of staff inability.
