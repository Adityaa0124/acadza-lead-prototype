# Entrance tests

**Route:** /admissions/cbt. **Role:** test staff/manager. Explain Computer-based test (CBT) in description.

Tabs: Ready to schedule, Scheduled, Results, Needs attention. Ready rows show student/grade, parent, readiness date and Schedule test. Scheduled rows show test/time/location and attendance action. No-show belongs to Needs attention.

Schedule dialog: student summary, test, date/time/timezone, mode/location, responsible staff if required. No need for decorative calendar dashboard. Reschedule keeps previous booking history and requires reason.

Record attendance through named actions. Mark no-show creates a contact/reschedule action, never automatic loss. Result form validates score/maximum or links the existing test result. Progression decision is separate from score.

Hand over to Admissions shows receiving responsibility and next action. Test direct ready entry, no slots, reschedule, no-show, pending result and unsuccessful progression.
