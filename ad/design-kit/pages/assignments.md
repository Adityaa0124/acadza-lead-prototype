# Assignments

**Route:** /admissions/assignments. **Role:** manager. **Primary action:** assign waiting enquiries when present.

A single notice identifies overflow: “200 enquiries are waiting for a caller”. Below, workload table: caller, availability, active/capacity, overdue and row action. Use numbers beside a small bar; no decorative gauges.

Unassigned list shows student, waiting since, reason and selection. Selection opens Assign selected with target workloads. Reassignment dialog shows affected count, target, required reason and explicit preservation of callback times. Show partial failures separately.

Pause new assignments does not remove existing work. On-leave staff remain visible for coverage decisions. All-capacity-full state explains why allocation stopped and offers an authorised management action.

Test balanced allocation, uneven load, capacity overflow, absence, conflicting bulk updates and reassignment with callback. Do not show a success count until confirmed by service.
