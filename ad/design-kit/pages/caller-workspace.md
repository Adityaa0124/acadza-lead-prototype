# My calls

**Route:** /admissions/my-work. **Role:** caller. **Primary action:** Save and next.

## Default layout

Desktop: 264px queue and flexible conversation workspace, 24px gap. The workspace groups context, notes and outcome in one reading order. No permanent third panel. At tablet/mobile open one lead at a time with Back to calls.

Queue header “Calls to make”; filter tabs Due, New and Upcoming. Due contains overdue and currently due work with visible appointment labels. Counts are service-derived. A manager's approved queue-priority policy determines ordering; due appointments must remain discoverable.

Rows show student/grade, parent and due reason/time. Active row has pale-blue fill and blue left line. Avoid multiple coloured badges on each row. Next lead never offers future appointments prematurely.

## Conversation hierarchy

1. Parent name and large phone with labelled Call and Copy.
2. Student/grade and current stage in one compact line.
3. Callback commitment if present, including time, timezone and parent's request.
4. Last relevant conversation with author/date and full readable note.
5. “What happened?” choice group: No answer, Call later, Interested, Ready for test.
6. More outcomes expands visit, not interested, admitted elsewhere, wrong number.
7. Conditional next-action fields and one optional note field.
8. Save and next primary; Save without moving secondary.

No outcome is preselected. Call later reveals date/time immediately. Parent-requested times are not silently changed. More outcomes remains expanded when one is selected. Wrong number with an alternative offers trying the alternative before invalidation.

## Recovery

Save failure leaves the record and note in place. A stale/closed record stops changes and explains why. The phone link only opens a calling app; it does not prove a call happened. Empty queue shows next upcoming commitment, not a chart.

## Acceptance

Test keyboard-only callback, long note, no alternate phone, busy parent, overdue task, session refresh, save failure and reassignment. Staff should complete a callback without opening any modal or remembering another page.
