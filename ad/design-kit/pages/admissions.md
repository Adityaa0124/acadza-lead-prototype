# Admissions

**Route:** /admissions/admission-queue. **Role:** admissions staff/manager.

List students with decision, remaining action, responsible person and due time. Default filter is active work. Opening a record reveals contact, decision evidence and school-defined milestones.

Milestone rows have label, current state, recorder/time and named update action. Do not imply documents/payments were verified when only a demo checkbox changed.

Complete admission confirms the agreed required information; missing items are stated plainly. Closure requires reason. Completed record shows date/actor and removes incompatible open tasks.

Test pending documents, no owner, completion, closure and reopened application. The definition of admission completed remains a school policy decision.
