# Login — Acadza brand entrance

**Status:** first prototype ready for review  
**Audience:** students initially; the pattern can later adapt to staff roles.

Desktop is a two-panel layout. The left panel holds the real task: Acadza wordmark, credentials card, slow orange/gold perimeter beam, recovery action, and support link. The right panel is brand theatre with a navy grid, a dot-built gear mark, and interactive `ACADZA` 3D wordmark.

Hovering or keyboard-focusing the dotted gear runs a single staggered flip from white to gold. The gear makes a small spring rotation and emphasises selected gold dots. Its circular inner cutout follows the approved Acadza mark. Reduced-motion users retain the mark and wordmark without animation.
