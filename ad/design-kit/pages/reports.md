# Reports

**Route:** /admissions/reports. **Role:** authorised manager.

Start with year/cohort and date controls; source/grade in Filters. Explain scope in a sentence. Show four compact figures at most: valid enquiries, active, admission completed, closed without admission.

Use a readable stage/count table or restrained horizontal bars. Include direct-to-test entrants so counts do not imply every lead was called. Provide source conversion and lost reasons below, not multiple decorative charts.

Rates show numerator/denominator definitions. Caller attempts are labelled self-reported unless verified by telephony. Unknown comparisons do not produce invented growth arrows.

Empty state says no data for filters. Export uses current filters and permissions. Test zero denominator, reopened leads and invalid/duplicate exclusions.
